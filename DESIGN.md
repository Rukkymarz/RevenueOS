Keep the Cyber Executive identity: #080B12 canvas, cyan #00E5FF, violet #7C3AED and mint #10B981. Preserve the AI orb, command composer, grouped navigation, themes, truthful metrics and approval controls.

## Applied references

- VoltAgent/awesome-design-md: Linear guide for precise typography, restrained panel borders and hierarchy; Stripe guide for tabular financial values and atmospheric light. Adapt principles, never third-party logos, proprietary fonts or marketing claims.

A fine orbital grid behind the AI Core, layered panel surfaces, a clear accent edge on metrics, illustrated empty records, and consistent 44px controls. Decorative elements are hidden from assistive technology. Respect light theme, reduced motion and small screens. No invented charts or sample customer records.
